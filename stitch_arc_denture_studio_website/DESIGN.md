## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury prosthodontic aesthetic centered on three tenets: Confidence, Craftsmanship, and Comfort. Instead of evoking the cold, anxiety-inducing, sterile cyan tones of generic dental clinics, the visual experience feels like a discreet private atelier or bespoke wellness residence. 

The aesthetic combines **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Architectural** sensibility. Warm ivory backdrops, tailored deep forest green tones, and delicate champagne bronze accents signal uncompromising precision, handcrafted artistry, and human warmth. 

The target audience encompasses mature, discerning patients seeking specialist restorative care, bespoke dentures, and implant-supported solutions. The interface must inspire dignity, quiet authority, and total physical and emotional ease, replacing medical vulnerability with transformative confidence.

## Colors

The palette directly references bespoke handcraftsmanship and natural organic warmth:

- **Primary (`#1B3B36` & `#0E2924`):** Deep Forest Green and Deep Cypress Teal drawn directly from the atelier logomark. It anchors hero elements, primary interactive triggers, and prestigious brand touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#C8A97E` & `#D6BA92`):** Muted Champagne Bronze. Used with restraint for micro-accents, active indicator borders, badge strokes, and selected highlights.
- **Tertiary / Base Neutral (`#161817` & `#1E2220`):** Rich Obsidian Charcoal. Selected in place of pure digital black to yield softer, high-contrast editorial typography that preserves visual comfort for mature eyes.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF8F5` & `#F5F2EB`):** Warm Ivory and Alabaster. Replaces clinical white, reducing glare and setting an atmosphere of hospitality and calm.
- **Structural Borders & Outlines (`#EBE6DC` & `#E3DCCE`):** Soft Stone and Sand. Establishes structural definition without heavy visual noise.

## Typography

The typographical pairing pairs high-end editorial heritage with legible, modern accessibility:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Elegant, transitional high-contrast serifs with refined terminal arches. Evokes tailored craftsmanship and medical mastery. Display and large headings use negative tracking to heighten bespoke poise.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A geometric humanist sans-serif offering open apertures, tall x-heights, and clear letterforms. Body sizes start at a comfortable 16px (scaling to 18px for primary narratives) to ensure legibility for mature clientele without eye strain.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted in uppercase with generous positive tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to impart an organized, architectural clarity across category pills, steps, and subheaders.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an unhurried, rhythmic fixed-to-fluid grid that mimics editorial publishing and spatial architecture. Whitespace serves as an active luxury material rather than void.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column structure constrained to a maximum content width of 1280px with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) lateral margins. Mobile views adapt to a 4-column flow with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins.
- **Rhythm & Density:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` and above) frame case studies, specialist credentials, and consultation steps. Content is never packed tightly; breathing room reflects patient comfort and surgical focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Layering** and **Subtle Ambient Shadows** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Layering Principle:** Interactive containers and cards sit atop the primary alabaster surface (`#FAF8F5`) using pure ivory sheets (`#FFFFFF`) or soft stone planes (`#F5F2EB`), bounded by fine 1px strokes in `#EBE6DC`.
- **Shadow Signature:** Shadows are warm, diffused, and low-opacity, tinted with deep cypress tones (`rgba(27, 59, 54, 0.04)`) to evoke soft daylighting across bespoke materials. Elevation ranges from flat borders at rest to soft, deep diffusion on hovered treatment cards (`0 16px 36px -8px rgba(27, 59, 54, 0.06)`).
- **Subtle Glass Accents:** Consultation booking bars and floating contact triggers utilize frosted ivory translucency (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` with a 16px backdrop blur) to maintain site awareness without visual interruption.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary takes inspiration from the organic contours of the natural dental arch ("Arc"):

- **Corner Radii:** Set to level `2` (Moderate Roundedness). Component corners employ soft, anatomical curvature (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, expanding to `1rem` / `16px` on clinical treatment modules and diagnostic cards).
- **Specialty Silhouette ('The Arc Contour'):** Feature cards, hero portrait imagery, and testimonial vignettes can selectively apply an asymmetric continuous curve along one quadrant (e.g., top-right or top-left radius at `48px` to `64px`) directly mimicking dental and palatal symmetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Green background (`#1B3B36`), crisp alabaster text (`#FAF8F5`), 12px vertical by 28px horizontal padding, radius 8px (`0.5rem`). Subtle hover elevates to deep cypress (`#0E2924`) with a soft warm bronze glow.
- **Secondary (Atelier Outline):** Transparent fill with a 1.5px border in `#1B3B36` or `#C8A97E`, text in `#1B3B36`, shifting to a delicate warm bronze tint on interaction.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Obsidian text with a bottom border indicator in `#C8A97E` that gently expands on focus.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Clean stone background (`#FFFFFF`), outlined with `#EBE6DC` at 1px thickness. Generous height (min 52px) for effortless touch input. Focus transitions the border to forest green (`#1B3B36`) accompanied by a subtle 2px champagne ring (`rgba(200, 169, 126, 0.25)`).
- **Helper & Validation Text:** Clear, high-contrast neutral obsidian subtext with accessible error and confirmation indicators.

### Cards & Service Folios
- **Treatment & Consultation Cards:** Built on crisp `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F2EB`, bounded by `#EBE6DC`. Feature an elevated header segment with Playfair Display titles and Plus Jakarta Sans specs. Interactive cards scale upward with a 1px border shift to bronze (`#C8A97E`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Specialization Tags:** Pill-shaped (`9999px` radius) chips rendered in warm ivory with `#E3DCCE` borders and tracked uppercase micro-typography (`label-sm`). Selected state activates a dark forest green background with ivory text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Accessible Indicators:** Sized at 22px for effortless engagement. Unselected elements show a clean `#EBE6DC` stone frame. Selected states present a deep `#1B3B36` center with a warm bronze confirmation checkmark or radio dot.

### Specialized Practice Modules
- **Before & After Interactive Sliders:** Minimalist hairline divider in bronze (`#C8A97E`) flanked by ivory arch pill badges.
- **Consultation Stepper:** Horizontal connected node system in bronze and forest green, designed to guide prospective patients step-by-step through their bespoke smile consultation.
